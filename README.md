# Customer Churn & Retention Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Customer Churn & Retention Analytics Dashboard** built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze customer churn patterns, retention trends, and customer characteristics. The dashboard helps identify factors associated with customer churn based on contract type, internet service, payment method, tenure, and monthly charges.

## Project Overview

Customer churn is an important business metric that reflects the percentage of customers who stop using a company's services. Understanding churn patterns can help businesses identify customer segments that may require retention strategies.

This project uses interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs) to explore customer behavior and compare customers who churned with those who stayed.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Customer Overview
Provides a summary of overall customer performance through key metrics and visualizations.

- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Churn Rate
- Average Tenure
- Average Monthly Charges
- Customer Distribution by Churn Status
- Churn Rate by Contract Type
- Customer Distribution by Tenure Group

### 2. Customer Detail
Displays detailed customer-level information to support further analysis and exploration of individual customer records.

- Customer information and service characteristics
- Contract and payment method details
- Tenure and monthly charges
- Customer churn status
- Interactive filtering by customer attributes

## Key Analysis

The dashboard explores several factors associated with customer churn:

- **Churn Status:** Comparison between customers who churned and those who stayed.
- **Contract Type:** Analysis of churn rates across different contract types.
- **Internet Service:** Examination of churn patterns by internet service.
- **Payment Method:** Comparison of churn rates across payment methods.
- **Tenure Group:** Analysis of churn behavior across customer tenure segments.
- **Monthly Charges:** Comparison of monthly charges between churned and retained customers.
- **Customer Characteristics:** Exploration of churn patterns based on customer attributes.

## How to Use

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the `.pbix` file using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. If prompted, update the dataset path or reconnect the data source.
4. Refresh the data if necessary.
5. Explore the dashboard pages and use the available filters to analyze customer churn patterns.
